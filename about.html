Common Questions

How often should my lawn be mowed?

For most residential lawns, we recommend weekly service during the peak growing season. This ensures we never remove more than one-third of the grass blade at once, which keeps the root system healthy and vibrant.

How do you handle persistent weeds in the yard?

We use a multi-step approach: first, we identify the weed type (broadleaf or grassy). We then apply targeted pre-emergent treatments in early spring and spot-treat any breakthroughs with eco-safe solutions throughout the year to keep your turf clean.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes, we provide detailed, no-obligation quotes for all landscaping and hardscaping projects.

Are you licensed and insured?

Absolutely. We are fully bonded, licensed, and insured for your peace of mind and ours.

How long does a typical patio installation take?

Most standard brick patios are completed within 3 to 5 business days, depending on the site prep required.
